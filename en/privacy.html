DATA & PRIVACY

Privacy, clearly explained.

How Anna Eliseeva uses information from enquiries.

Information you choose to share

When you send an enquiry through the site, the server stores your name, contact details, topic, message and company name if provided.

Purpose of contact details

Enquiries and emails are used to respond and discuss potential cooperation. Access is limited to the site owner and authorised hosting administrators. CVs and other documents are only sent if you choose to share them.

Candidate information

Candidate CVs are not published on this website. Their transfer to clients and retention need to be covered under separate terms and procedures.

Third-party services

The site uses system fonts and no third-party website analytics. Choosing an email link can trigger processing by your email provider under its own terms.

Enquiry retention

Enquiries submitted through the website form are automatically deleted one calendar month after receipt.

Privacy enquiries

For data-related questions or deletion requests, email annaeliseeva70057@gmail.com.
